the existing MODEL.md laws and source conventions. Expected values were not

Chromium at 390×844 and 1024×844, then operated discharge gates, pump modes
and the tank clock through the UI. This is a scoped quality review, not
field calibration or validation of every supported combination.

CGBVP and the independent Waterous cross-check). No duplicate UI, geometry
or law tests were added. Temporary browser scripts and worksheets are audit
artifacts in `/tmp/hydraulic-quality/`, not additions to the test suite.

hand arithmetic; scenario inputs use metres. Numerical comparisons passed

## Defects corrected

1. **Shared-feed flow in the inspector and fittings.** At 200 gpm in a
   C = 2 feed, the solver correctly returned 8 psi friction, but its caption
   said “at 100 gpm”, using the selected terminal's flow. Captions now read
   the local flow from the engine's section/fitting terms. A separate
   380 gpm feed with two 190 gpm branches confirms that its source adapter
   correctly says the 10 psi stepped allowance applies; branch flow must
   not be used to choose that explanation.
2. **Derived smooth-bore ratings.** An imported 1″ tip could retain a stored
   100 gpm field while the solver correctly used 210.011 gpm at 50 psi.
   The inspector, Concepts explanation and illustrative stream now use the
   engine's derived rating. Imports and laws are unchanged.
3. **Downhill credit on mobile.** The compact budget showed nozzle + friction
   exceeding source pressure without its desktop explanation. It now states
   the elevation credit explicitly: 115.32 psi from the pump + 8.68 psi from
   descent supplies the 124 psi represented by the positive slices.
4. **Short tank endurance.** Positive time under roughly three seconds could
   round to `0.0 min`. The tank panel and strip now show whole seconds below
   one minute, rounding up; empty, unknown and no-consumption labels stay
   distinct.

The suspected unavailable-flow inconsistency did not reproduce in the main
results: the page already replaces those calculations with an unavailable
operating-point explanation. That guard remains in place.

## Operating checks

- Closed connected nozzles retain static pressure; isolated branches show
  unknown pressure. The surviving branch and feed update after a gate closes.
- Closing one of the two independent CX discharges changes delivery from
  1000 total at 202 psi to about 518.9 gpm at 217.5 psi. Reopening restores
  the two-outlet point. Pressure and fixed-RPM controls give their declared
  behaviour; the control is shaft rpm, not engine rpm.
- In the browser, a 10 gal tank at 100 gpm reaches approximately 4.9 gal
  after the three-second wait and the pause click (the click adds elapsed
  time). It stays unchanged while paused, reaches exactly zero after resume,
  and refill returns it to 10 gal with the clock paused and delivery restored.
- A live pressure change from 124 to 31 psi changes flow from 100 to 50 gpm
  while the tank clock runs. Remaining water agrees with piecewise elapsed
  volume accounting within 0.2 gal, allowing for browser interaction timing.
- Both viewport sizes were checked. The scenario sweep reported no page
  errors or horizontal page overflow. Final targeted checks cover the changed
  captions, smooth-bore classification, mobile credit and short endurance.
